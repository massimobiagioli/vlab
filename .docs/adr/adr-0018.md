# ADR-0018: Allow the BSD-3-Clause and Zlib transitive licenses

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Supersedes: —
- Superseded by: —

## Context

WP-2 adds the SQLx SQLite driver and the strict YAML parser, which bring two
transitive licenses that the `deny.toml` allowlist did not cover:

- `encoding_rs` 0.8.44 (`(Apache-2.0 OR MIT) AND BSD-3-Clause`), reached
  through `encoding_rs_io` ← `serde-saphyr`.
- `foldhash` 0.2.0 (`Zlib`), reached through `hashbrown` ← `hashlink` ←
  `sqlx-core`.

Both are OSI-approved, permissive, and compatible with the project's own
MIT OR Apache-2.0 licensing. `cargo deny check` rejects them as "not
explicitly allowed"; `AGENTS.md` §7 forbids ignoring or suppressing the
finding, so the policy must explicitly accept a license the project has
reviewed.

## Decision

Add `BSD-3-Clause` and `Zlib` to the `[licenses] allow` list in `deny.toml`.
Every other license remains denied. The two crates are used unmodified and
are not relicensed.

## Consequences

- The license gate passes on the actual dependency graph while still rejecting
  copyleft and unknown licenses.
- Any future crate under either license is permitted. Both are permissive and
  impose only attribution obligations, which the project satisfies by keeping
  the dependency metadata and license texts in the dependency sources.
- The `option-ext` MPL-2.0 exception remains narrow and unchanged
  (ADR-0012).

## Alternatives considered

- **Ignore the two crates in `cargo deny`**: rejected; an ignore hides future
  license changes and is a weaker policy than reviewing the license.
- **Replace `serde-saphyr` or SQLx**: rejected; the licenses are acceptable,
  and replacement would trade a real, reviewed license for a larger change.
- **Relax the gate to warnings**: rejected by `AGENTS.md` §7.
